NETFLIX CONTENT ANALYSIS — KEY INSIGHTS
========================================

Dataset:
The dataset contains 8,807 Netflix titles, including 6,131 Movies
and 2,676 TV Shows.

1. MOVIES VS TV SHOWS
---------------------
Movies make up approximately 69.6% of the Netflix catalog, while TV
Shows account for approximately 30.4%. This shows that the catalog
is significantly more movie-focused than TV-show-focused.

2. TOP COUNTRIES
----------------
The United States is the largest contributor to the Netflix catalog
by a significant margin, followed by India and the United Kingdom.
This indicates a strong concentration of content from the US market,
while India is also a major contributor.

Note: Some country values contain multiple countries in the same
cell, such as "United States, India", so these results represent
country entries rather than perfectly separated country counts.

3. TOP DIRECTORS
----------------
Rajiv Chilaka has the highest number of titles in the director
analysis, followed by "Raul Campos, Jan Suter" and Suhas Kadav.
This shows that a relatively small group of directors account for
a large number of titles in the dataset.

Note: Some rows contain multiple directors in a single cell, so
the analysis counts director entries rather than individually
separating every director.

4. TOP GENRES
-------------
Drama is one of the most heavily represented genres in the Netflix
catalog, along with International Movies and Comedies. The catalog
therefore has a strong presence of drama, comedy, and international
content.

Multiple genres were separated using split() and explode() before
counting them individually.

5. CONTENT ADDED BY MONTH
-------------------------
July has the highest number of Netflix titles added among the months
in the dataset. Content additions are therefore not evenly
distributed throughout the year.

6. CONTENT BY RELEASE YEAR
--------------------------
Netflix's catalog is heavily concentrated around more recent
content, with the late 2010s showing particularly high numbers of
titles. This suggests that the catalog has a strong emphasis on
modern releases rather than older content.

7. MOVIES VS TV SHOWS OVER TIME
-------------------------------
Movies remain the dominant content type overall, but TV Shows show
strong growth in the later years. This indicates that Netflix has
increasingly expanded its TV content alongside its movie catalog.

8. CONTENT RATINGS
------------------
TV-MA and TV-14 are among the most common ratings in the dataset.
This indicates that a large portion of Netflix's catalog is intended
for mature or teenage audiences.

9. NETFLIX CONTENT ACQUISITION
------------------------------
Netflix's content additions increased substantially during the late
2010s compared with earlier years in the dataset. This indicates a
major period of catalog expansion during that period.

This analysis uses date_added, which represents when Netflix added
the title, rather than when the title was originally released.

10. MOVIE DURATION
------------------
Most Netflix movies are concentrated around the 90–120 minute range,
with the average movie duration being around 100 minutes. This
suggests that the catalog largely follows conventional feature-film
runtime patterns.

11. TV SHOW SEASONS
------------------
Most TV Shows in the dataset have relatively few seasons, with
1–2-season shows making up a large portion of the catalog. This
suggests that Netflix's TV catalog contains many relatively
short-running series rather than being dominated by long-running
shows.

OVERALL CONCLUSION
------------------
Netflix's catalog is predominantly movie-focused and heavily
weighted toward recent content. The United States is the largest
content contributor, while India is another major contributor.
Drama, comedy, and international content are strongly represented.
Although Movies remain dominant, TV Shows have grown considerably
over time. Netflix also experienced a major period of content
expansion during the late 2010s, while most movies follow
conventional feature-length runtimes and most TV Shows have
relatively few seasons.
